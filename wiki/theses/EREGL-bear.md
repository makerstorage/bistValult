# EREGL — bear thesis

## Narrative

EREGL's Q1 2026 results show extremely thin profitability with only $9 million net profit on $1.4 billion revenue, a 0.6% margin that leaves minimal buffer against cost inflation [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]]. The company's 2026 CapEx guidance of $600 million signals a heavy investment cycle that will require either debt issuance to 1.8x net debt/EBITDA or equity financing [[claims/eregl-2026-capex-600m-guidance]]. With capacity utilization already at 96%, volume growth upside is limited, making EREGL vulnerable to demand softening or global steel price volatility [[claims/eregl-q1-2026-strong-domestic-demand-high-utilization]].

- [[catalysts/strong-domestic-demand]] — if domestic demand weakens and utilization falls below 85%, bear thesis strengthens

## What would invalidate it

- Q2 2026 or Q3 2026 results showing net profit margin expanding above 5% (vs. 0.6% in Q1 2026)
- EBITDA per ton rising sustainably above Q1 2026 levels as guided, demonstrating pricing power
- Management demonstrating ability to pass through energy or raw material cost increases to customers
- Successful execution of $600M CapEx investments generating returns above cost of capital
- Global steel prices rising 15%+ from Q1 2026 levels, improving revenue per ton
