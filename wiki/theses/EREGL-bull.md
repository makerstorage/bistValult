# EREGL — bull thesis

## Narrative

EREGL demonstrates strong operational momentum with 96% capacity utilization and $1.4 billion in Q1 2026 revenue driven by robust domestic steel demand [[claims/eregl-q1-2026-strong-domestic-demand-high-utilization]]. Management guides EBITDA per ton to rise in Q2 2026, suggesting potential margin expansion [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]]. The $600 million CapEx program and 1.8x net debt-to-EBITDA target for 2026 signal a commitment to operational improvements and disciplined capital allocation [[claims/eregl-2026-capex-600m-guidance]].

## Supporting claims

- [[claims/eregl-q1-2026-strong-domestic-demand-high-utilization]] — 96% capacity utilization, $1.4B revenue, $137M EBITDA in Q1 2026 with strong domestic sales
- [[claims/eregl-2026-capex-600m-guidance]] — $600M CapEx guidance and 1.8x net debt/EBITDA target for 2026

## Key risks to the thesis

- [[risks/global-steel-price-volatility]] — declining global steel prices would directly reduce EREGL's revenue and EBITDA margins
- [[risks/energy-cost-exposure]] — rising energy costs would compress the 9.8% EBITDA margin reported in Q1 2026

## Catalysts that would strengthen it

- [[catalysts/rising-ebitda-per-ton-q2-2026]] — management guidance for EBITDA per ton improvement in Q2 2026
- [[catalysts/strong-domestic-demand]] — continued high capacity utilization above 90% in subsequent quarters

## What would invalidate it

- Q2 2026 EBITDA per ton below Q1 levels, contradicting management guidance from [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]]
- Capacity utilization falling below 90% in Q2 or Q3 2026, suggesting domestic demand weakness
- Net debt/EBITDA exceeding 2.0x at year-end 2026, indicating capital allocation slippage from the 1.8x target [[claims/eregl-2026-capex-600m-guidance]]
- Q2 or Q3 2026 revenue declining >10% quarter-over-quarter
