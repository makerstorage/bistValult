# Renewable Energy

## What it is

The build-out of wind, solar, and energy storage capacity in Turkey, driven by government policy, energy security goals, and decarbonization targets. Also includes grid infrastructure investment that enables renewable integration.

## Current state

Fact (2026-04-30): ENTRA received positive EIA for a 49.4 MW storage-integrated wind project in Tekirdag, with three further storage-integrated projects still in EIA process. [[sources/2026-04-30-reuters-ic-enterra-yenilenebilir-says-it-receives-positive-environme-89bbbee3]]

Fact (2026-04-28): EUPWR unit Peak PV Solar won TEIAS tender worth TRY 331.3 million, indicating active grid infrastructure investment. [[sources/2026-04-28-reuters-europower-enerji-says-unit-wins-teias-tender-worth-331-3-mln-f863e6cc]]
