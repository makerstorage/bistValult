# FX (Foreign Exchange)

## What it is

Turkish lira (TRY) exchange rate movements against major currencies (USD, EUR), affecting companies with foreign-currency revenues, costs, debt, or receivables.

## Current state

Fact (as of 2026-04-22): The Turkish lira hovered near a record low of 44.92 per USD amid US-Iran ceasefire uncertainty and broad EM currency weakness. [[sources/2026-04-22-reuters-stocks-fx-dip-on-us-iran-ceasefire-uncertainty-focus-on-cenb-63df0012]]

Fact (as of 2026-04-30): TSKB guides on "FX-adjusted loan growth" basis, indicating FX movements are material to reported loan book metrics. Low-teens FX-adjusted loan growth guided for 2026. [[sources/2026-04-30-reuters-tskb-maintains-2026-guidance-fbd41a7f]]
