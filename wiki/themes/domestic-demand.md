# Domestic Demand

## What it is

The level of Turkish domestic consumer and business spending, influenced by inflation, interest rates, credit availability, and real incomes.

## Current state

Fact (as of 2026-04-30): Turkish domestic tractor market is contracting sharply — TTRAK revised market size expectation to 20,000–26,000 units for 2026, and cut domestic sales guidance to 8,000–10,000 from 11,500–14,500. [[sources/2026-04-30-reuters-turk-traktor-q1-net-result-turns-to-loss-of-1-3-bln-lira-yoy-b9458561]]

Fact (Q1 2026): DMLKT sold 1,623 residential units for TRY 29.9 billion total revenue; no YoY comparison available. [[sources/2026-04-29-reuters-emlak-konut-reit-says-total-sales-revenue-from-sale-of-resid-0e9d68a2]]

Interpretation: Mixed signals — domestic tractor demand collapsing while natural gas and steel demand appear to be holding (CWENE, AHGAZ, ENERY, ISDMR all show strong YoY growth). Suggests sector-specific rather than economy-wide demand patterns.

## Companies benefiting

- [[companies/CWENE]] — Q1 2026 net profit ~doubled YoY; revenue +31% YoY suggests strong domestic energy engineering demand
- [[companies/AHGAZ]] — FY 2025 revenue +15% YoY in natural gas distribution
- [[companies/ENERY]] — FY 2025 revenue +6.5% YoY in energy distribution
- [[companies/ISDMR]] — Q1 2026 revenue +18% YoY in steel

## Companies hurt

- [[companies/TTRAK]] — severe domestic tractor market contraction driving revenue down ~39% YoY
- [[companies/AKCNS]] — flat revenue and widening losses in cement
- [[companies/AFYON]] — marginally growing revenue but swinging to net loss

## Related

- Risks: Consumer credit tightening; inflation eroding purchasing power
- Catalysts: Policy rate normalization; wage growth

## Open questions

- Broader indicators of Turkish consumer demand (retail sales, credit card spend) not available in current sources.
- Whether the construction sector decline is broad-based or sector-specific.
