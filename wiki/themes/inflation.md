# Inflation

## What it is

High and persistent consumer price inflation in Turkey, which drives nominal revenue growth while simultaneously inflating costs (wages, energy, raw materials, finance costs), pressuring real margins.

## Current state

Fact (as of 2026-04-30): Turkish inflation remains elevated enough that TSKB guides "above average CPI OPEX growth" for 2026, indicating CPI remains a significant cost driver for corporates. [[sources/2026-04-30-reuters-tskb-maintains-2026-guidance-fbd41a7f]]

## Companies benefiting

- [[companies/ANSGR]] — premium repricing inflates non-life technical income faster than claims in short term
- [[companies/CIMSA]] — profit improvement on flat revenue suggests inflation-driven pricing power or cost discipline

## Companies hurt

- [[companies/TKNSA]] — finance costs and operating expenses rising faster than revenue; net losses persist
- [[companies/TUKAS]] — ~79% profit decline on flat revenue suggests cost inflation outpacing pricing
- [[companies/AKCNS]] — widening losses with flat revenue
- [[companies/AFYON]] — swing to loss (TRY 73.4M loss vs TRY 115.4M profit Q1 2025) on modest revenue growth
- [[companies/ULUUN]] — persistent losses in flour milling, narrowing but still negative
- [[companies/TSKB]] — above-average CPI OPEX growth guided for 2026
- [[companies/TTRAK]] — domestic demand suppressed, likely partly by inflation-driven purchasing power erosion

## Related

- Risks: Real consumer demand compression; finance cost escalation
- Catalysts: Policy rate normalization leading to easing
