# Claim: AFYON's Q1 2026 net result swung sharply to a loss despite modest revenue growth, suggesting severe cost pressure

## Statement

AFYON's Q1 2026 net result swung from a profit of TRY 115.4 million (Q1 2025) to a loss of TRY 73.4 million, while revenue grew only 3.1% YoY — implying cost inflation significantly outpaced revenue growth.
