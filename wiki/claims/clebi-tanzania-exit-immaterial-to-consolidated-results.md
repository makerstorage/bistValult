# Claim: CLEBI's Tanzania exit is immaterial to consolidated financials and eliminates a net loss-contributing unit

## Statement

CLEBI's exit from Tanzania (Celebi Tanzania Aviation Services at Julius Nyerere International Airport) following concession expiry will not materially impact consolidated financials, as the unit represents only ~0.2% of revenue and ~0.5% of assets while contributing ~2.1% to net loss drag.

## Evidence

- [[sources/2026-04-24-reuters-celebi-hava-servisi-says-it-will-exit-tanzania-operations-af-0c9c4874]] — "THE UNIT… HAS A LIMITED CONTRIBUTION TO CONSOLIDATED RESULTS, ACCOUNTING FOR ABOUT 0.2% OF TOTAL REVENUE AND 0.5% OF TOTAL ASSETS… WHILE ITS LOSS IMPACT ON NET PROFIT STOOD AT AROUND 2.1%… THE EXIT WOULD NOT HAVE A MATERIAL IMPACT ON CONSOLIDATED FINANCIALS OR PROFITABILITY"

## Supports

- Supports a view that the Tanzania exit is a minor but slightly margin-positive event for CLEBI.
