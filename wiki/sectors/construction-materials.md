Fact (2026-04-24): AKCNS proposed dividend of TRY 1.88 gross per share from FY 2025 profit, pending general assembly approval. [[sources/2026-04-24-reuters-akcansa-cimento-says-it-proposes-to-pay-cash-dividend-at-gro-42308c20]]

Fact (as of 2026-03-31): CIMSA Q1 2026 net profit jumped to TRY 640.7 million (from TRY 355.1 million Q1 2025); revenue flat at TRY 11.6 billion. [[sources/2026-04-30-reuters-cimsa-cimento-q1-net-profit-jumps-to-640-7-mln-lira-yoy-7b386d9f]]

Fact (as of 2026-03-31): AFYON Q1 2026 net result swung to loss of TRY 73.4 million (from profit TRY 115.4 million in Q1 2025); revenue grew marginally +3.1% YoY to TRY 926.1 million. [[sources/2026-04-28-reuters-afyon-cimento-q1-net-result-turns-to-loss-of-73-4-mln-lira-y-52626a50]]

Interpretation: Mixed Q1 2026 results across cement sector — CIMSA profit nearly doubled while both AKCNS and AFYON swung to or deepened losses on modest revenue growth. This suggests company-specific cost structure or product mix differences rather than uniform sector dynamics. Warrants further research into cost drivers for loss-making producers.
