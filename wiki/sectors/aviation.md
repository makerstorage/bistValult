# Aviation

## Members in universe

## Drivers

- International air traffic volumes
- Concession and contract renewals at airports

## Risks

- Concession expiry risk (as illustrated by Tanzania exit)
- Geopolitical disruption to international travel flows

## Sector-level facts

Fact (2026-04-24): CLEBI will exit Tanzania operations (Julius Nyerere International Airport) following concession expiry. Tanzanian unit accounts for ~0.2% of consolidated revenue, ~0.5% of assets, and ~2.1% of net loss drag. Exit described as immaterial to consolidated financials.
