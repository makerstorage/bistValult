# High CapEx cycle

## What it is

EREGL's management guided 2026 full-year CapEx at $600 million with a net debt to EBITDA target of 1.8x, signalling a heavy investment cycle that strains cash flows and increases leverage.

## Severity

Medium — as of 2026-05-02, based on [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]].

## Companies exposed

- [[companies/EREGL]] — $600 million CapEx guidance represents ~4.4x Q1 2026 EBITDA ($137M), requiring either debt issuance (targeting 1.8x net debt/EBITDA) or equity financing, both of which carry costs.

## What would change the severity

- CapEx guidance being reduced would lower severity.
- Successfully executing projects that increase EBITDA/ton would reduce severity.
- Inability to generate returns above cost of capital would increase severity.
