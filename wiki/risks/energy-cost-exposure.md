# Energy cost exposure

## What it is

Steel production is energy-intensive, requiring significant electricity and natural gas inputs. Energy price inflation directly compresses margins for producers like EREGL.

## Severity

Medium — as of 2026-05-02, based on [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]].

## Companies exposed

- [[companies/EREGL]] — EREGL's Q1 2026 net profit of only $9 million on $1.4 billion revenue (0.6% margin) shows minimal buffer to absorb energy cost increases.

## What would change the severity

- Sustained decline in Turkish energy prices would reduce severity.
- Successful energy efficiency investments would reduce severity.
- Sharp energy price increases would increase severity.
