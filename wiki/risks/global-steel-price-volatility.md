# Global steel price volatility

## What it is

Steel prices are subject to global supply-demand imbalances, trade policy changes, and macroeconomic cycles, creating revenue and margin volatility for producers like EREGL.

## Severity

Medium — as of 2026-05-02, based on [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]].

## Companies exposed

- [[companies/EREGL]] — EREGL's $1.4 billion quarterly revenue and $137 million EBITDA are directly exposed to steel price fluctuations; 96% capacity utilization leaves little volume buffer to offset price declines.

## What would change the severity

- Sustained global steel price increases would reduce severity.
- Trade tariffs or protectionist policies restricting exports would increase severity.
- Global recession reducing steel demand would increase severity.
