# Valuation watchlist

Names where current evidence suggests a notable gap between price and intrinsic value — in either direction. Inclusion is **not** a buy/sell signal; it flags pages that warrant a closer read.

## Inclusion criteria

A ticker enters the watchlist when at least one `[[claims/...]]` page asserts a valuation gap with a cited source. Removed (or moved to "resolved") when:

- the claim is invalidated by a newer source, or
- the price closes the gap, or
- a `[[decisions/...]]` page supersedes the watchlist note.
