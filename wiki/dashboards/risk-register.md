## How this page is maintained

- New row when a `[[risks/<name>]]` page is created.
- `Severity` is qualitative (Low / Medium / High) and reflects current evidence; revise on contradiction or new source.
- `Companies exposed` lists every `[[companies/TICKER]]` page whose body links the risk.
- A risk is retired (moved to a `## Retired` section) when no live thesis depends on it.
