# 2026-04-24 — Celebi Hava Servisi Will Exit Tanzania Operations After Airport Concession Expiry

## Provenance

- **Raw file:** `raw_sources/news/2026-04-24-reuters-celebi-hava-servisi-says-it-will-exit-tanzania-operations-af-0c9c4874.md`
- **Publisher / author:** Reuters
- **Original date:** 2026-04-24
- **Ingested:** 2026-04-30

## Key facts

- Fact: CLEBI's Tanzanian subsidiary Celebi Tanzania Aviation Services will cease operations following the expiry of the concession agreement at Julius Nyerere International Airport in Dar es Salaam (2026-04-24).
- Fact: The Tanzanian unit provides ground handling and air cargo services (2026-04-24).
- Fact: The Tanzanian unit accounts for approximately 0.2% of CLEBI's total consolidated revenue and 0.5% of total assets as of 2025-12-31.
- Fact: The Tanzanian unit's loss impact on consolidated net profit stands at approximately 2.1% as of 2025-12-31.
- Fact: CLEBI decided not to pursue operations in the Tanzanian market after a comprehensive evaluation, in line with its long-term value creation strategy (2026-04-24).
- Fact: The exit is not expected to have a material impact on consolidated financials or profitability (2026-04-24).
- Fact: CLEBI maintains operational presence across Europe, Asia and Africa, and recently entered the Kenyan market (2026-04-24).
