# 2026-04-30 — Otokar Says It Signs Agreement To Buy 96.77% Of Romania's Automecanica For EUR 85 Mln

## Provenance

- **Original date:** 2026-04-30
- **Ingested:** 2026-04-30

## Key facts

- Fact: Otokar Otomotiv ve Savunma Sanayi (BIST: OTKAR) signed a share purchase agreement to acquire 96.77% of Romania-based defence company Automecanica for approximately EUR 85 million (2026-04-30).
- Fact: This agreement follows a Memorandum of Understanding disclosed on 2026-01-27.
- Fact: The final purchase price will be determined at closing, subject to net debt and working capital adjustments.
- Fact: Otokar paid EUR 5 million at the time of the MOU and a further EUR 5 million upon signing the share purchase agreement.
- Fact: EUR 25 million will be retained as a guarantee for sellers' obligations, paid in installments over three years post-closing; remaining balance paid at closing.
- Fact: Transaction is subject to approvals from Romania's Competition and Foreign Direct Investment authorities; may be extended by up to three months if conditions are not met.
- Fact: Automecanica will become a direct subsidiary of Otokar and will be used to support the Romanian 4x4 tactical wheeled light armored vehicle procurement tender.
