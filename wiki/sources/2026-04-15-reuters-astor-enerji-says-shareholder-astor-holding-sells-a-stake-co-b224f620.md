# 2026-04-15 — Astor Enerji Says Shareholder Astor Holding Sells A Stake Corresponding To 5.99% Of Company's Share Capital

## Provenance

- **Raw file:** `raw_sources/news/2026-04-15-reuters-astor-enerji-says-shareholder-astor-holding-sells-a-stake-co-b224f620.md`

## Key facts

- Fact: Main shareholder Astor Holding sold 5.99% stake in Astor Enerji (ASTOR) corresponding to 59.8 million shares at 187.5 lira per share (2026-04-15).
- Fact: Sale completed via accelerated bookbuilding on Tuesday; gross proceeds 11.2 billion lira; priced at ~7.95% discount to previous closing price (2026-04-15).
- Fact: Shares sold to institutional investors outside Turkey; Citigroup Global Markets and HSBC acted as joint global coordinators and bookrunners (2026-04-15).
- Fact: Transaction expected to settle on April 20, 2026, subject to regulatory approvals; to be executed as wholesale trade on Borsa Istanbul (2026-04-15).
- Fact: Post-sale, Astor Holding stake expected to fall to ~0%; public float projected to rise to ~42.75% (2026-04-15).
- Fact: Feridun Gecgel expected to continue holding ~57.25% of company and remain majority shareholder (2026-04-15).
