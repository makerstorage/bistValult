# 2026-04-30 — IC Enterra Yenilenebilir Says It Receives Positive Environmental Impact Assessment Decision For Akcahalil Wind Power Project In Tekirdag

## Provenance

- **Raw file:** `raw_sources/news/2026-04-30-reuters-ic-enterra-yenilenebilir-says-it-receives-positive-environme-89bbbee3.md`

## Key facts

- Fact: IC Enterra Yenilenebilir Enerji (BIST: ENTRA) received a positive Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) decision for its storage-integrated Akcahalil Wind Power Project in Tekirdag Province (2026-04-30).
- Fact: The Akcahalil project will consist of 7 turbines with installed capacity of 49.4 MW, electric output of 45 MW, and energy storage capacity of 45 MW / 45 MWh (2026-04-30).
- Fact: EIA processes for three other storage-integrated projects are still under way as of 2026-04-30.
