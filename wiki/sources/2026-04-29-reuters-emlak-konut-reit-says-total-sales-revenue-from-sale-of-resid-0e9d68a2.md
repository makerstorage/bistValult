# 2026-04-29 — Emlak Konut REIT Says Total Sales Revenue From Sale Of Residential Units And Land Amounted To 29.9 Bln Lira, from January 1 to March 31

## Key facts

- Fact: Emlak Konut REIT (BIST: DMLKT) sold 1,623 independent units generating TRY 24.3 billion (excluding VAT) in pre-sale value from January 1 to March 31, 2026 (2026-04-29).
- Fact: DMLKT sold 25 plots for TRY 5.5 billion in the same Q1 2026 period (2026-04-29).
- Fact: Total DMLKT Q1 2026 sales revenue from residential units and land was TRY 29.9 billion (2026-04-29).
- Fact: DMLKT sales to foreign nationals in Q1 2026 amounted to 21 units for TRY 305.8 million (excluding VAT) (2026-04-29).
