## Key facts

- Fact: Gen Ilac ve Saglik Urunleri (BIST: GENIL) targets commissioning of its Azerbaijan pharmaceutical production facility in October 2026 (2026-04-29).

- Fact: GENIL performance has exceeded its 30% TRY-based growth target set at the beginning of 2026 (2026-04-29).
- Fact: GENIL Phase-1 studies for the SUL-238 project have been completed; Phase-2 studies have commenced (2026-04-29).
- Fact: GENIL GN-037 project for psoriasis has completed Phase-2 studies with positive results (2026-04-29).
