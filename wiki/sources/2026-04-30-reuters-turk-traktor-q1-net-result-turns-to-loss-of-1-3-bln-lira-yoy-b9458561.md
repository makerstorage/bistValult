# 2026-04-30 — Turk Traktor Q1 Net Result Turns To Loss Of 1.3 Bln Lira YoY, Revises 2026 Guidance

- **Raw file:** `raw_sources/news/2026-04-30-reuters-turk-traktor-q1-net-result-turns-to-loss-of-1-3-bln-lira-yoy-b9458561.md`

## Key facts

- Fact: TTRAK (BIST: TTRAK) reported Q1 2026 net loss of TRY 1.3 billion versus net profit of TRY 309.2 million in Q1 2025 (2026-04-30).
- Fact: TTRAK Q1 2026 revenue was TRY 10.1 billion versus TRY 16.6 billion in Q1 2025 (2026-04-30).
- Fact: TTRAK revised 2026 domestic tractor sales guidance to 8,000–10,000 units from prior 11,500–14,500 units (2026-04-30).
- Fact: TTRAK maintains 2026 tractor exports guidance at 11,000–13,500 units (2026-04-30).
- Fact: TTRAK revised 2026 capex guidance to USD 60–70 million from prior USD 75–100 million (2026-04-30).
- Fact: TTRAK revised Turkish tractor market size expectation to 20,000–26,000 units from prior 27,000–33,000 units for 2026 (2026-04-30).
