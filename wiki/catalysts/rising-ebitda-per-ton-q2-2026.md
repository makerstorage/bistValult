# Rising EBITDA per ton in Q2 2026

## What it is

Management guidance that EBITDA per ton will rise in Q2 2026 compared to Q1 2026, suggesting improving margins or product mix.

## Companies affected

- [[companies/EREGL]] — EREGL reported $137 million EBITDA in Q1 2026; rising EBITDA per ton would increase quarterly EBITDA at similar volume levels.
