# Strong domestic demand for steel

## What it is

Turkish domestic steel demand remains robust, driving high capacity utilization and revenue for EREGL.

## Companies affected

- [[companies/EREGL]] — EREGL operated at 96% capacity utilization in Q1 2026 with domestic sales described as "strong," generating $1.4 billion in quarterly revenue.

## Status

Active — as of 2026-05-02, cited [[sources/2026-04-29-quartr-eregl-strong-q1-results-with-rising-ebitda-per-ton-and-robus-829c608f]]. Q1 2026 results demonstrate strong demand.

## What would change the status

- Capacity utilization falling below 85% in subsequent quarters.
- Management revising domestic demand outlook downward.
- Turkish construction or manufacturing activity contracting sharply.
