# Emlak Konut REIT Says Total Sales Revenue From Sale Of Residential Units And Land Amounted To 29.9 Bln Lira, from January 1 to March 31

Emlak Konut Gayrimenkul Yatirim Ortakligi BIST:DMLKT:

Emlak Konut Gayrimenkul Yatirim Ortakligi :

EMLAK KONUT REIT - FROM JANUARY 1 TO MARCH 31, 1,623 INDEPENDENT UNITS WERE SOLD, GENERATING 24.3 BILLION LIRA (EXCLUDING VAT) TOTAL PRE-SALE VALUE

EMLAK KONUT REIT - IN THE SAME PERIOD 25 PLOTS WERE SOLD FOR VALUE OF 5.5 BILLION LIRA

EMLAK KONUT REIT - TOTAL SALES REVENUE FROM SALE OF RESIDENTIAL UNITS AND LAND AMOUNTED TO 29.9 BILLION LIRA

EMLAK KONUT REIT - SALES TO FOREIGN NATIONALS AMOUNTED TO 21 UNITS AND 305.8 MILLION LIRA (EXCLUDING VAT)
