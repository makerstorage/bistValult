# Tofas Signs Loan Agreement Amounting To EUR 275.7 Mln, In Connection With K9 Commercial Vehicle Contract

TOFAŞ TÜRK OTOMOBİL FABRİKASI BIST:TOASO: Source text for Workspace:  Further company coverage:

TOFAŞ TÜRK OTOMOBİL FABRİKASI :

SAID ON FRIDAY SIGNS LOAN AGREEMENT WITH BANCO BILBAO VIZCAYA ARGENTARIA, S.A. (BBVA) FOR AN EXPORT CREDIT AGENCY (ECA) LOAN AMOUNTING TO 275.7 MILLION EUROS, INCLUDING INSURANCE PREMIUM IN CONNECTION WITH K9 COMMERCIAL VEHICLE CONTRACT

THIS LOAN WILL SUPPORT FINANCING OF PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED INVESTMENT OF 256 MILLION EUROS

LOAN IS EXPECTED TO BE UTILIZED WITHIN ONE MONTH AND WILL MATURE IN DECEMBER 2034

BASED ON ESTIMATED DISBURSEMENT SCHEDULE AND AVERAGE MATURITY, WITH SEMI-ANNUAL PRINCIPAL AND INTEREST PAYMENTS, ANNUAL TOTAL COST OF THE LOAN WILL BE IN RANGE OF 6-MONTH EURIBOR + 2.25% – 2.35%
