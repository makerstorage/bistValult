The BIP050 governance proposal asks whether BIM should structure its fiat on/off-ramp so that fiat redemptions do not translate into direct BIM sell orders on the open market, as outlined in the DAO vote announcement on X. Mechanically, this would likely involve using internal inventory, hedging, or alternative liquidity sources instead of market-selling BIM when users convert to fiat. If approved and credibly implemented, this could reduce visible sell pressure on spot markets and improve order book resilience, which traders may interpret as supportive for BIM’s price during periods of heavy fiat outflows.
