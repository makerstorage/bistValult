# IC Enterra Yenilenebilir Says It Receives Positive Environmental Impact Assessment Decision For Akcahalil Wind Power Project In Tekirdag

IC ENTERRA YENİLENEBİLİR ENERJİ BIST:ENTRA:Source text for Workspace:Further company coverage:

IC ENTERRA YENİLENEBİLİR ENERJİ :

SAID ON WEDNESDAY RECEIVES A POSITIVE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT (EIA) DECISION FOR ITS STORAGE-INTEGRATED AKCAHALIL WIND POWER PROJECT IN TEKIRDAG PROVINCE

COMPANY SAYS THE AKCAHALIL PROJECT WILL CONSIST OF SEVEN TURBINES WITH AN INSTALLED CAPACITY OF 49.4 MW, ELECTRIC OUTPUT OF 45 MW, AND ENERGY STORAGE CAPACITY OF 45 MW / 45 MWH

EIA PROCESSES FOR ITS OTHER THREE STORAGE-INTEGRATED PROJECTS ARE STILL UNDER WAY
