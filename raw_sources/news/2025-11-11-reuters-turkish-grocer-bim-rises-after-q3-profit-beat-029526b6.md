** Shares in BIM Magazalar  rise more than 5% after the Turkish discount store chain posted better-than-expected Q3 profit

** BIM reports Q3 net profit of 5.27 billion lira ($125.6 million), beating consensus by 16%, according to analysts from J.P. Morgan and Deniz Yatirim; EBITDA was 19% above consensus, JPM adds

** "The positive surprise was driven by stronger than expected operating margins as gross margin remained resilient in Q3 at close to peak levels, and the adverse impact of staff cost adjustments faded quicker than anticipated," JPM says

** Brokerage Deniz Yatirim highlights the company's strong EBITDA performance despite financial pressures

** The BIM stock is the third best performer on Turkey's main BIST 100 index
