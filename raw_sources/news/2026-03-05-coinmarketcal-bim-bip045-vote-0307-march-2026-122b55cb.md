According to the BIP045 governance announcement on X, the BIM DAO is voting on whether the Yecho Buyback Dashboard becomes the official tracker for BIM token buybacks (source). The mechanism does not change the buyback policy itself but standardizes how buyback data is reported and verified. Clear, on-chain-aligned tracking can make it easier for traders to quantify how much BIM is being retired from circulation over time, which directly affects effective float and potential valuation. Strong voter turnout and passage would signal active governance and reinforce the buyback narrative around BIM.
