** Shares in Turkish retailer BIM Birlesik Magazalar  drop 5.8% after Turkey's competition board imposes a $36 million fine on it

** The Turkish Competition Authority said on Wednesday it had decided to impose an administrative fine of around 1.3 billion lira ($35.8 million) on BIM as a result of obstruction of an on-site inspection

** The discount store chain says it will take legal action once officially notified of the fine

** Serhat Baskurt, algorithmic operations manager at brokerage ALB Yatirim, views the situation as a warning signal for other retailers

** "In the short term, I anticipate sell-off pressure to continue," Baskurt adds

** The BIM stock is the worst performer on Turkey's BIST-100 index  which is up 0.23%
