Here are news, reports and events that may affect Turkish financial markets on Wednesday.

The lira  was quoted at 43.8400-43.8750 against the U.S. dollar at 0410 GMT, after closing at 43.8400 on Tuesday.

The BIST 100 index closed at 14,050.83 points on Tuesday, down 0.08% from the previous session's close.

GLOBAL MARKETS

Asia's financial markets were higher on Wednesday with Korean chipmakers leading gains, as investors double down on what they see as the safest bet on artificial intelligence (AI), while the yen remained in focus.

ERDOGAN PROGRAMME

President Tayyip Erdogan will make a speech in parliament to lawmakers from his ruling AK Party at their weekly meeting. (0900 GMT) He will also speak at a fast-breaking dinner at the presidential palace in the evening. (1542 GMT)

PARLIAMENT SPEAKER

Parliament Speaker Numan Kurtulmus will visit the parliamentary groups of the AK Party (1300 GMT) and the New Path Party (1200 GMT).

ASELSAN RESULTS

Defence company Aselsan said its net profit rose to 29.95 billion lira ($683 million) last year from 20.02 billion lira the previous year.

MAIN OPPOSITION PARTY

Ozgur Ozel, leader of the main opposition Republican People's Party (CHP), will visit jailed Istanbul mayor Erkem Imamoglu in prison (0800 GMT), before speaking at a party rally in Istanbul's Bakirkoy district. (1700 GMT)

PARLIAMENTARY MEETINGS

The IYI Party (0730 GMT) and the New Path Party (0630 GMT) will hold their parliamentary group meetings.
