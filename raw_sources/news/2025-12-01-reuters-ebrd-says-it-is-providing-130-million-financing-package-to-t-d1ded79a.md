The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) said on Monday it will provide $130 million in funding to Turkey's Akbank  to bolster the financing of inclusive growth and digital investments for small and medium-sized enterprises in the country.

In a statement, EBRD said the package included support under three of its financing programmes: for women-led SMEs, youth-led SMEs, and to support investments by Turkish manufacturing SMEs in digital transformation.
